## Severity Levels
- **Critical** — app crashes or data loss
- **High** — feature broken, no workaround
- **Medium** — feature partially broken
- **Low** — minor UI/UX issue

## Status Options
- **Open** — not yet fixed
- **In Progress** — being worked on
- **Fixed** — resolved
- **Closed** — verified and closed
